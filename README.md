  $$m_0 = \frac{h}{2\pi r_{2o} c}$$

### 2. Invariance of Local Action
The localized angular action ($\mathcal{S}$) of the internal sub-rotations is a fundamental invariant of nature, tied to Planck's constant ($h$).

### 3. Dynamic Action-Velocity Conservation
Total energy is conserved via a fluid-like mechanical trade-off. If external field gradients underload or overload the invariant internal rotational action ($h$), the vortex must instantly extract from or deposit to its forward translational action (macroscopic velocity) to maintain structural equilibrium.
