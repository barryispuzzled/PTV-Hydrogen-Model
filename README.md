## 📐 Foundational Axioms (Mathematical Framework)

### 1. The Three-Tier Nested Rotation Matrix
Fundamental particles are non-singular, localized photon filaments spinning along a nested three-dimensional geometric loop matrix:
* **Sp-1 (String Polarization):** Local helical rotation. The string travels at a total speed of $\sqrt{2}c$ with a fixed rake angle of $\pi/4$, establishing equal parts circular velocity ($c$) and translational velocity ($c$).
* **Sp-2 (Orbital Angular Momentum Loop):** The closed loop that dictates rest mass ($m_0$) geometrically relative to its physical radius ($r_{2o}$):
  $$m_0 = \frac{h}{2\pi r_{2o} c}$$
* **Sp-3 (Toroidal Closing Loop):** Bends the Sp-2 axis into a closed toroid (donut shape), creating an internal self-potential and setting the ground state toroidal speed to match the fine-structure constant ($\alpha c$).

### 2. Invariance of Local Action
The localized angular action ($\mathcal{S}$) of the internal sub-rotations is a fundamental invariant of nature, tied to Planck's constant ($h$).

### 3. Dynamic Action-Velocity Conservation
Total energy is conserved via a fluid-like mechanical trade-off. If external field gradients underload or overload the invariant internal rotational action ($h$), the vortex must instantly extract from or deposit to its forward translational action (macroscopic velocity) to maintain structural equilibrium.
